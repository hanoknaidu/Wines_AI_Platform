# Free Stream Guide: Business Goals

*Goals inferred from the page's content, copy, and structure. The HTML does not state them explicitly, so please confirm them with the product owner.*

## Purpose

Free Stream Guide ("streamguide") is a single-page curated directory that helps people find **free, legal, ad-supported streaming services** without needing a subscription or credit card.

## Primary Goals

1. **Reduce the cost of entertainment for users.** Point users to services that need no subscription and no card ("All ad-supported, no subscription, no card required").
2. **Position the site as a trusted, legal-only source.** The "FREE & LEGAL ONLY" tagline signals reliability and keeps clear of piracy-related risk.
3. **Cut the time it takes to find something to watch.** Group 20 service entries across four categories (Movies & TV, News, Sports, Entertainment), so users reach the right service in one or two clicks.
4. **Send qualified traffic to partner services.** Every card has a clear "Open [Service] ↗" call to action that opens the service in a new tab, which makes it a natural base for referral or affiliate partnerships.

## Secondary Goals

5. **Set honest expectations.** The copy flags weak spots (for example, "the weakest free category" for sports, and an antenna recommended for live local sports), which builds credibility and reduces user disappointment.
6. **Help users pick the right kind of service.** Badges (ad-supported, library card, national, local, FAST channels, 250+ channels, no account needed) let users compare options at a glance.
7. **Serve different viewing intents.** Separate on-demand viewing (Movies & TV) from channel-surfing (Entertainment) and live information (News).
8. **Encourage repeat visits.** The note "Catalogs rotate monthly — check back often" gives users a reason to return.

## Audience

- Cost-conscious viewers and cord-cutters
- People who want a free alternative to paid streaming
- Library cardholders (Kanopy, Hoopla)
- Users looking for free local and national news

## Suggested Success Metrics

| Goal | Possible KPI |
|---|---|
| Drive qualified traffic | Outbound click-through rate per card |
| Fast discovery | Time to first click; category tab switches per session |
| Repeat visits | Returning-visitor rate (monthly) |
| Partnership value | Clicks per partner; referral conversions, if applicable |
| Content freshness | Date of last catalog review; number of broken links |

## Risks and Open Questions for Stakeholders

- **Content accuracy:** Catalog details (such as "50,000+ movies" and "250+ channels") change often, so the page needs a regular review process.
- **Service changes:** Some services listed may have been rebranded or merged (Freevee in particular should be verified), and links may break.
- **Monetization:** The page has no analytics, affiliate tracking, or ads yet.
- **Reach and discoverability:** There is no search, no SEO metadata beyond the title, and no personalization.
- **Maintenance:** The content is hard-coded in HTML, so every update means editing the file by hand.
